# Why Schools & Institutions Choose Vyoma

Vyoma empowers schools, educators, and students with India's most advanced NEP 2020-aligned smart educational ecosystem.

## Key Institutional Advantages

1. **Increase Engagement**: Immersive gamified pathways and media that keep students actively curious and engaged.
2. **AI-Powered Learning**: Tailored AI tutoring that adapts in real-time to each student's learning velocity and unique requirements.
3. **Interactive 3D & VR Lessons**: Visualize complex science and math topics with photorealistic 3D models and VR environments.
4. **Teacher Dashboard**: Live classroom analytics, customized lesson planners, and automated grading aids for teachers.
5. **Parent Progress Reports**: A transparent portal showing student concepts mastered, strengths, and recommended improvements.
6. **Exam Readiness**: Rich mock test generators, interactive practice runs, and revision paths to boost confidence.
7. **Skill Development**: Fosters practical critical thinking, conceptual problem-solving, and future digital skills.
8. **NEP 2020 Aligned**: Fully certified and designed in alignment with national education policies and competency structures.
